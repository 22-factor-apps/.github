# Code of Conduct

We want rigorous disagreement without personal hostility. Be specific about
claims, generous about intent, and willing to revise a position when evidence
changes. Critique ideas and implementations rather than people or identity.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, or disclosure of private security and assessment material are not
acceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove harmful contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owners through GitHub.
Maintainers will minimize disclosure, avoid conflicts of interest where
possible, and communicate the outcome to the reporting person.
